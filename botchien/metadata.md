## Description
🔥 Bột Chiên — Saigon Fried Rice-Flour Cake: steamed rice-flour cubes fried golden in a black cast-iron pan, covered with egg and scallions, served with pickled papaya and a sweet soy-vinegar sauce at a street cart in Saigon at dusk.
🔥 Bột chiên Sài Gòn – bột hấp cắt miếng chiên giòn trên chảo gang, phủ trứng, hành lá, ăn kèm đồ chua và nước tương chua ngọt bên xe đẩy vỉa hè lúc chạng vạng.

🇬🇧 HOW IT'S MADE
1. Mix rice flour, tapioca starch and water
2. Steam it into a soft white slab
3. Cut it into cubes
4. Fry in hot lard in a cast-iron pan
5. Toss until golden & crispy
6. Crack the eggs over the top
7. Spread the egg and let it set
8. Flip the whole cake!
9. Shower with scallions
10. Pickled green papaya & carrot on the side
11. Sauce: soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, chili
12. Plate on banana leaf and drizzle — crispy outside, chewy inside
