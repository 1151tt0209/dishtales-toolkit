# PLAN: Bánh xèo miền Tây (Mekong Sizzling Crêpe), 69.6 s, vertical 1080×1920

Folder `D:\DishTales-hoathinh\banhxeo\`. Toolkit copied from `hutieu/`: lib, props, people, text, render, shots, nprops, hprops + hp_*.

## Look: golden hour → sunset at a riverside stilt house (new for the river series)
- Earlier river videos: nem nướng = pink misty sunrise on the market; hủ tiếu = hard 8 AM sunshine, blue sky. This one is **5 PM golden hour sliding into sunset**.
  - Sky: deep amber-orange near the sun, magenta and violet clouds higher up. The sun is low and big on the horizon, behind coconut palms. Long, warm rim light.
  - River: copper water with a long orange sun path. Palms, nipa palm (dừa nước) and the far bank in purple silhouette.
- **Nhà sàn ven kênh**: wooden stilt house over the canal, nipa-thatch roof, bamboo-slat walls, a deck with a low table, a small wooden ladder down to a moored xuồng. Clay pots and a herb garden in styrofoam boxes and old basins on the bank.
- **Kitchen inside**: dark, lit by a red wood fire under a clay stove (bếp lò đất / ông táo) with 2 small cast-iron pans. Sunset beams through the bamboo slats (god rays) cut through smoke and steam. Hanging baskets, ladles, garlic braids.
- In act III–IV, lanterns and a string of warm bulbs switch on along the deck (dusk).

## Music: **swing manouche "Mekong gypsy jazz", 125 BPM** (new BPM and style)
- Swing 8ths. Acoustic guitar "la pompe" comping, upright bass walking 2-feel → 4-feel, brushes on snare, finger snaps on 2 and 4.
- Lead: clarinet + **đàn kìm** (moon lute, plucked, Southern-scale ornaments, Vọng cổ-flavoured licks). Violin answers in act II.
- **The sizzle is an instrument**: a "XÈÈO" hiss swell lands on the backbeat when batter hits the pan. Spatula on the pan = rimshot, lid clank = crash.
- CTA jingle: clarinet glissando up + kìm tremolo + a "ba-dum-tss" rimshot on the wink.
- Grid: **1 beat = 0.48 s**, 1 bar = 1.92 s. Total **145 beats = 69.6 s**. All cuts on beats.

## Characters
- **Má Tư** (new): Southern grandmother cook. Grey hair in a bun, **maroon áo bà ba**, checked khăn rằn over her shoulder, warm smile, wooden spatula. Use `coUt`/`diBay` with a new palette + grey bun.
- **Tourist** (series regular): blond, sunglasses pushed up, turquoise tee, backpack. Arrives in a small xuồng rowed by the boatman, then sits on the deck.
- **Chú lái xuồng** (boatman): grey tee, bucket hat, paddles by again in act III.
- **Ducks**: a little flock on the canal (comic chorus).
