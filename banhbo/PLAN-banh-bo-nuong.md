## Cast and setting
- **Cô Út** (Mekong vendor, bà ba tím, nón lá): `NP.coUt`, `NP.coUtFace` (see header of `nprops.js`; `bunmam/river.js`, `nemnuong` used her). She runs a small riverside sweets stall: thatched shelter, wooden table, clay charcoal oven (`lò than`: round clay pot with a lid, coals below and ON the lid), banana-leaf and pandan bundles.
- **Tourist** (blond, sunglasses on head, turquoise tee, backpack): always `people.js` `tourist/face/eyeMacro/thumbsUp` (series look). Expressions needed: curious, sniffing (eyes closed), chewing a leaf, fogged-glasses, polite knife-and-fork, stretched strand, happy bite, thumbs-up.
- Palette: warm gold light + fresh pandan green accents (`#7fbf4a` → `#3f8f3a`), cake crust `#a8642a`→`#c98a3c`, interior `#9ccf5a` with darker green tunnels. No black outlines, soft gradient shadows, big eyes (same style as the rest of the series).

## The cake (must look the same in every scene)
Round dome (bundt-ish, diameter ≈ 560 px at s7), golden-brown crust with a few darker blisters, a thin crack ring at the top. Cut/torn face: **honeycomb**: tall vertical tunnels (2–14 px wide in a 1080 frame at normal zoom, longer toward the top), walls light pandan green `#b5dc6a`, tunnel interiors darker `#6fa63a`, a slightly glossy sticky surface (small white highlights). Torn pieces show **sticky strands** (thin, slightly translucent light-green threads with a bright highlight, sagging in a catenary and thinning in the middle).

## Shared contract
- Signature `function sN(ctx, lt, p, T)`: paint the **full 1080×1920 frame, opaque**. render.js adds grade, vignette, flashes (24.231, 28.846, 36.923, 46.731, 57.115), grain and all text. No text in shots (except UI on the CTA buttons).
- Camera helper that never shows outside the plate: `const cam = (ctx,cx,cy,z,rot,fn) => L.cam(ctx, clamp(cx,540/z,W-540/z), clamp(cy,960/z,H-960/z), z, rot, fn);` (see `demo15/shots.js`; **cy ≠ 960 without clamping shows black bars**).
- Cache static layers at module level; no per-frame big canvases; blur only on cached layers. < 0.3 s/frame.
- Reuse libraries: `bxprops.js` (`BX.NP` = river world / Cô Út, `BX.HP`), `bx_env/food/fx/chars.js`, `people.js`, `lib.js`. **Read the API headers first.** New props (mould, oven, honeycomb cake, strands, pandan leaf, mortar…) go in your own new file. Don't edit shared files except tiny backward-compatible fixes (list them).
- Preview with `node render.js --sheet <t1,t2,…> <prefix>_<name>.png --notext` (prefix `a_` / `b_`), **open every PNG with the Read tool**, check start/middle/end and every key event time, fix and iterate 2–3 rounds. Also one sheet without `--notext` to make sure captions do not hide the hero.
- Known weak points from earlier videos (be better): cartoon hands/faces look cheap in close-up, leaf wraps look like tubes, food that looks flat or like plastic. Give the cake real volume: gradients, subsurface-like glow in the green, glossy sticky highlights.
- Report back briefly: shots done, preview paths, weaknesses, any shared-file fixes, and the **exact times of the events you actually animated** (so audio can be re-synced).
