## Look: Saigon blue hour, 5 AM, turning into sunrise (new)
- Earlier Saigon videos: bánh tráng trộn = golden hour; bột chiên = soft dawn street. This one starts in **deep blue hour** (cobalt/indigo sky, sodium-orange street lamps, a **pink + cyan neon "CƠM TẤM" sign**, tangled power lines) and the **sun rises during Act II** (peach/rose sky by s14, warm sunlight shafts through the charcoal smoke by s21).
- Location: a narrow Saigon alley (hẻm) with old tube houses, potted plants, laundry, a bougainvillea, red plastic stools, a stainless-steel cart with glass case. **Charcoal smoke** drifting through lamp light everywhere (the signature).
- Light: orange charcoal glow from below + cool blue ambient + neon pink/cyan rim light. Smoke is lit by the neon.
- Palette: cobalt/teal + neon magenta/cyan + ember orange → peach sunrise. Must not look like cá lóc (moonlit field) or the gold bánh xèo.

## Music: **Saigon city-pop / disco-funk, 112.5 BPM** (new BPM and style)
- 4/4, four-on-the-floor kick, 16th hi-hats, open hat on the off-beats, handclaps on 2 and 4. Slap/octave bass (funky), Rhodes/electric piano 7th chords (Maj7, m9), chicken-scratch muted guitar, a bright **synth brass stab** riff, string swells, a lead synth (square/saw, slight portamento) for the hook. Key: D major / B minor (city-pop changes: IVmaj7–V/IV–iii7–vi7).
- **Grill as an instrument**: sizzle hiss swells on the flips, the fan "whup-whup" in tempo, tongs click as percussion, a motorbike passing (doppler) in Act I, a rooster at dawn.
- Comedy: frantic fanning speeds up the hats, a big smoke **POOF** (filtered noise boom + cymbal) at 45.867, then **silence 47.467–48.533** (only a soft crackle), a cartoon **cough** "KHỤ!" at 49.067, a sad trombone-ish synth "wah-wah" when he's revealed with a soot face.
- CTA jingle: a disco string-run up + brass stab + cowbell, a "ding" on the FOLLOW press, a sparkly chime on the wink.
- Grid: **1 beat = 8/15 s (0.5333)**, 1 bar = 2.133 s. Total **128 beats = 68.267 s** (2048 frames). All cuts on beats.

## Characters
- **Cô Năm** (new): Saigon street vendor in her 50s, round friendly face, hair in a low bun with a clip, **floral áo bà ba** (lilac with small white flowers), a dark-green apron, rubber sandals, a straw hand-fan (quạt nan) and long tongs. Warm, quick, a little sassy.
- **Tourist** (series regular): blond, sunglasses on his head, turquoise tee, khaki shorts, a backpack in Act I. Clumsy and enthusiastic.
- Extra: a passing motorbike, a cat on the stool, a rooster silhouette on a rooftop at sunrise.

## The dish (accurate)
- Broken rice (cơm tấm) — small, fluffy, white grains, steamed, mounded in a bowl shape on a plate.
- **Sườn nướng** (grilled pork chop, bone-in, thin, marinated with lemongrass, garlic, shallot, fish sauce, honey/sugar, condensed milk; caramelised, charred edges, glossy).
- **Bì** (shredded pork skin + thính / toasted rice powder).
- **Chả trứng** (steamed egg-and-pork meatloaf, yellow yolk layer on top, cut in a square slice).
- **Ốp la** (sunny-side-up fried egg).
- **Mỡ hành** (scallion oil, bright green).
- **Đồ chua** (pickled carrot + daikon), cucumber and tomato slices.
- **Nước mắm** (sweet fish sauce with chili and garlic) in a small bowl, poured over.
