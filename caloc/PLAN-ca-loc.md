Folder `D:\DishTales-hoathinh\caloc\`. Toolkit copied from `banhxeo/`: lib, props, people, text, render, shots, nprops, hprops + hp_*, bx_* (bánh xèo props stay available as `BX`).

## Look: moonlit flood-season field at night (new for the river series)
- Earlier river videos: nem nướng = pink misty sunrise; hủ tiếu = hard 8 AM sunshine; bánh xèo = golden hour → sunset. This one is **night in mùa nước nổi (flood season)**.
  - Sky: deep indigo/navy with a big pale-gold **full moon**, thin silver clouds, stars. Moonlight path on the water.
  - Flooded rice field: a flat sheet of water up to the horizon, rows of young rice tips poking out, a dike (bờ đê) with a dirt path, a few coconut palms and a lone stilt hut (chòi) in silhouette, a tiny canal with a xuồng.
  - **Fireflies** (đom đóm) everywhere in the dark — little green-yellow dots blinking.
  - Warm light sources only: Chú Ba's **head torch / oil lantern** for night fishing (soi cá), then the **rice-straw bonfire** (big orange fire, sparks, smoke) on the dike. Faces lit orange from below, rim lit blue by the moon.
- Palette: blue-teal night + orange fire. Must not look like the gold bánh xèo or the blue-sky hủ tiếu.

## Music: **"Mekong spaghetti western", 90 BPM** (new BPM and style)
- Straight 8ths, 4/4, A minor. Whistled melody (Morricone-style), twangy reverb baritone electric guitar (tremolo picking), bold trumpet lead, đàn bầu bends answering the whistle, đàn kìm plucks.
- Percussion: tom/kick heartbeat, clip-clop woodblocks, cabasa/shaker, whip-crack on accents, a big low gong. Night ambience: crickets, frogs, water lapping, fireflies (tiny chimes).
- **The fire is an instrument**: straw crackle rhythm bed, a big WHOOSH swell on the ignition (22.667), sparks as shaker.
- Comedy: slide-whistle down on the fall, a big SPLASH, silence underwater (bubbles only), then a frog "RIBBIT" on the beat and a trumpet "wah-wah".
- CTA jingle: whistle glissando up + guitar twang + a whip crack on the wink.
- Grid: **1 beat = 2/3 s (0.6667)**, 1 bar = 2.667 s. Total **105 beats = 70.0 s**. All cuts on beats.

## Characters
- **Chú Ba** (new): Southern rice farmer, 50s, tanned, thin moustache, **black áo bà ba** with rolled sleeves, checked **khăn rằn** tied round his head, rolled trousers, barefoot, a head torch (đèn đội đầu) at night, a bamboo fish trap (lờ / nơm) and a woven basket (giỏ cá) on his hip.
- **Tourist** (series regular): blond, sunglasses on head, turquoise tee, khaki shorts, backpack in Act I, holds a phone flashlight.
- **Fish**: cá lóc (snakehead) — dark grey-green back with mottled bands, pale belly, long body, round head with a big mouth. Very expressive for the comedy (wiggles, slaps, leaps).
- **Frog** (ếch) and a few **egrets / fireflies** as the comic chorus.
