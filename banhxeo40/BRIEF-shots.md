## Contract
- **Signature:** `function sN(ctx, lt, p, T)`. `lt` = local time in the shot (s), `p` = progress 0..1, `T` = global time. Paint the **full frame** with an opaque background. render.js wraps each call in save/restore.
- **Files:** `shotsA.js` exports s1..s9, `shotsB.js` exports s10..s16 (`module.exports = { s1, s2, … }`). Only write your own shot file. Don't edit shots.js, render.js, overlay.js, audio.js, or the other writer's file.
- **Helpers:** don't change existing exports in bx_*.js / hp_* / nprops / people / lib / props. New helpers (e.g. coconut heart strands, pork-belly slicing, shrimp on plate, crêpe broken in half, mustard-leaf roll) go inside your own shot file. Tiny backward-compatible bug fixes in bx_*.js are OK; list them in your report.
- **Grid:** 72 BPM, beat = 0.8333 s. Key sync points (global T): batter hits pan 0.3; whip pan 5.0; shrimp lands on plate 6.9; knife cuts 7.5 & 7.92; shrimp+pork into pan 9.3; edges meet on fold 16.667; match cut 17.5; crust cracks 22.5; dip 29.0; bite 30.833 (loudest moment); heart pops 36.667 and pulses every beat after; FOLLOW pressed 37.5; final frame holds still 39.5–40.0.
- **Continuity:** Má Tư (maroon áo bà ba, khăn rằn, `maTu`/hands) cooks in the dark riverside kitchen with the clay wood-fire stove and black cast-iron pan (`kitchenInterior`, `castPan`). The tourist (`tourist` from bx_chars / people.js series look: blond, sunglasses on head, turquoise tee) is the diner in scene 5. Same plate, herb basket and fish-sauce bowl from s10 onward.
- **Palette / light:** scenes 1–3 dark kitchen, red-orange fire glow + golden sunset beams through bamboo slats cutting steam; scene 4 warm golden hour on the wooden table; scene 5 late-afternoon gold outdoors; scene 6 sunset (`sunsetSky`/`riversideScene` with dusk ≈ 0.5–0.7), low sun rim light behind the crêpe, thin smoke drifting across.
- **Cinematic camera:** use `L.cam` for dolly-in, push, crane, arc/orbit, tracking; never hold a static frame for long (except the final 0.5 s hold). Fake depth: blurred foreground elements (leaves, steam, pan rim), bokeh in the background. Rack focus = animate blur between layers. Slow motion feel = ease.outExpo / very slow particle velocities in s1, s5, s11.
- **Performance:** < 0.3 s/frame. Cache static backgrounds at module level, never create big canvases per frame, ≤ ~300 particles per frame.
- **Verify by looking:** `node render.js --sheet <t1,t2,...> <prefix>_<name>.png --notext` writes `previews\<name>`. Use your own prefix (`a_` for shotsA, `b_` for shotsB) so the two writers don't overwrite each other. Check 3+ frames per shot (start, middle, end, plus every sync point) and **open the PNGs with the Read tool**. Fix: clipping, empty or flat areas, hands not reaching props, wrong scale between people/pan/plate/food, food that looks flat or like plastic, anything that reads as childish. Iterate 2–3 rounds. Also check one sheet WITHOUT `--notext` to confirm the hero subject isn't hidden behind the captions.
- **Report back (short):** shots done, the preview PNG paths, known weaknesses, any bx_* fixes.
