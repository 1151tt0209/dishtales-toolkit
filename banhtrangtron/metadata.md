## Description
🥭 Bánh Tráng Trộn — Saigon Rice Paper Salad: rice paper snipped into ribbons, tossed in a plastic bag with green mango, beef jerky, dried squid, quail eggs, chili oil, shrimp salt, calamansi, scallion oil, herbs and peanuts, then SHAKEN hard. Sold from a little glass cart outside every Saigon school gate at 4 PM.
🥭 Bánh tráng trộn Sài Gòn – bánh tráng cắt sợi, trộn trong bịch với xoài xanh, khô bò, khô mực, trứng cút, sa tế, muối tôm, tắc, mỡ hành, rau răm, đậu phộng, rồi LẮC thật đều. Món ăn vặt tuổi thơ trước cổng trường lúc 4 giờ chiều.

🇬🇧 HOW IT'S MADE
1. Snip the rice paper into ribbons
2. Into the plastic bag
3. Shredded green mango
4. Beef jerky and dried squid
5. Quail eggs
6. Chili oil (sa tế)
7. Shrimp salt
8. A squeeze of calamansi
9. Scallion oil
10. Vietnamese coriander, peanuts and fried shallots
11. Tie it and SHAKE the bag!
12. Top it off with more eggs and jerky
13. Serve with iced calamansi tea
